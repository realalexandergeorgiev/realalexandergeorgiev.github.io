BSides Women

A BSides Frankfurt–driven initiative focused on bringing more women into cybersecurity and strengthening connections across security communities.

Our goal is to encourage and support more women in technical security conferences - not only as attendees, but also as speakers and active contributors to the community.

Together with multiple women's communities, we create a welcoming space for networking, collaboration, and technical exchange.
