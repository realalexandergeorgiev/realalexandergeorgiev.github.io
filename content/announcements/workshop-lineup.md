We've confirmed the workshop lineup for **BSidesFrankfurt 2026**. This year features six hands-on sessions spanning offensive, defensive, and OT security:

- Hands-on Windows Internals (Dr. Anna Becker)
- Practical Binary Exploitation for 2026 (Tomás Weber)
- Cloud Attack Paths: AWS and Azure Case Studies (Isabelle Dupont)
- Detection Engineering from Scratch (Priya Nair)
- OT Security Fundamentals (guest instructor)
- Intro to CTFs: First-Timer Track (student-led)

Workshop tickets are sold separately and capped at 24 seats per session. Past editions have sold out in around three weeks.
