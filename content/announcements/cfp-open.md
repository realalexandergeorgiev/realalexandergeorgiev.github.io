The Call for Papers for **BSidesFrankfurt 2026** is now open. We're accepting submissions for 45-minute talks, 20-minute short talks, and 4 hour workshops (8 hour also possible).

Submissions close on **30 July 2026**. First-time speakers are especially encouraged to apply - we reserve slots for new voices at every edition.

Submit via [cfp.bsidesfrankfurt.org](https://cfp.bsidesfrankfurt.org).
