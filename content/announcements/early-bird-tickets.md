Early-bird tickets for **BSidesFrankfurt 2026** are now on sale at a reduced price. Quantities are limited - once the early-bird allocation is gone, standard pricing applies.

A **student rate** is also available with valid university enrollment, and a limited number of financial-aid tickets are reserved for applicants who cannot otherwise attend. Email [tickets@bsidesfrankfurt.org](mailto:tickets@bsidesfrankfurt.org) to apply.
