## TL;DR

- Every attendee gets a blank **ID-1 / CR80 card** alongside their lanyard.
- Grab the [SVG template](/files/2026-card-template.svg), design your card.
- Watch **X / LinkedIn / Mastodon** — the submission email drops in the coming weeks.

This September, your BSidesFrankfurt badge isn't the only thing you'll be wearing. Alongside your lanyard, every attendee receives a blank **ID-1 (CR80) card** — a small, keepable piece of the conference that you design yourself and take home at the end of the day.

## The card

The card follows the **ID-1 / CR80** format — the same size as a credit card, ID card, or business card: **85.60 × 54.00 mm**, with a **3.18 mm** corner radius. That's the only hard rule. Everything else — artwork, text, jokes, QR codes, your handle, your favourite CVE number — is up to you.

## Get yours printed — on us

Here's the deal: send us your finished design before conference week and we'll **print it and hand it to you on the day of the conference**. Guaranteed. No on-site queue, no printing fee, no "maybe".

We'll announce the **submission email address in the coming weeks**. Keep an eye on our channels so you don't miss it:

- **LinkedIn** — [BSides Frankfurt](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bsides-frankfurt/)
- **X / Twitter** — [@BSidesFRA](https://x.com/BSidesFRA)

Once the address is live, email us your final SVG or PNG at card size (300+ DPI recommended). We handle the rest, and your card will be waiting at the badge desk in September.
