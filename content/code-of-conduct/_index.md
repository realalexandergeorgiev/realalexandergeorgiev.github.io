---
title: "Code of Conduct"
description: "BSidesFrankfurt Code of Conduct - expected behaviour, reporting, and consequences."
---

BSidesFrankfurt is a community-run security conference. Everyone is welcome -
regardless of gender, age, nationality, religion, ethnicity, disability, or
experience level. We expect all participants to treat each other with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and professional
- Respect personal boundaries and different viewpoints
- Keep the event safe for everyone

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation in any form
- Unwanted physical contact or sexual attention
- Disruptive or threatening behaviour
- Unauthorised access, scanning, or attacks on conference infrastructure

## Consequences

Violations may result in removal from the event without refund and, if
necessary, involvement of law enforcement.

## Report an incident

Talk to any organiser on-site, or contact us at
[info@bsidesfrankfurt.org](mailto:info@bsidesfrankfurt.org). All reports are
handled confidentially.

---

Applies to all attendees, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers.
